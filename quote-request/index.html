Proposed SLA — informed by our time-zone coverage
With offices in Seattle, the UK and Melbourne each working standard business hours, requests are covered for roughly 23 of 24 hours in relay — Melbourne's day ends as the UK's begins, and the UK's day overlaps Seattle's morning. The only real gap is a narrow window in the UK's early morning (broadly 7–9am GMT, shifting slightly with daylight saving on each side). All targets below are subject to that coverage — a request landing right in the gap, or late on a Friday before a weekend, starts its clock from when an office actually opens, not the exact submission time.
This is a suggestion for review, not yet policy — Lisa's original guidance (72h quotes / 24h queries) still stands until confirmed otherwise. Reach out to Ajay if the team wants to adopt these figures.
Who does what
The three offices split responsibility as follows:
ORN EUEurope delivery — quotes, bookings, and the delivery quality control for the region: making sure what's promised in a quote is what actually gets confirmed and delivered on the ground.

ORN AustraliaRes, etc — reservations and related operational functions for the Australia desk.
